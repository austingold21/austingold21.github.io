Centric Brands Sales internship, 2026

License "Gap to Secure" model

Centric licenses brands like Disney and Universal, and each contract carries a minimum guarantee. I proposed and built a Python pipeline that consolidates the shortfall tracker, contract terms, and sales history into one ranking of how much each licensor still needs in sales to meet its guarantee.

Added contract-stage and required-annual-pace metrics so a large early-term gap isn't mistaken for a license at risk, and joined sales to retail channel data after reconciling naming mismatches across 20+ national retailers.

Grata Data analyst internship, 2025

Parent-company detection and domain classification

Analyzed over 1 million external links across roughly 197,000 company websites to find signals that reveal a company's parent organization, then built a weighted score from link frequency, URL structure, and acquisition keywords.

Found that acquisition language was the strongest signal: about two-thirds of sites using it linked to their parent company. A second analysis compared vendor data against Grata's database and flagged roughly 43,000 domains that were likely misclassified.

  • Python
  • SQL
  • Feature engineering
  • Correlation analysis

Rutgers Business School Team coursework

Financial distress classification for loan decisions

Built models to help a bank decide on loans by predicting which of 422 companies were financially distressed, a hard problem because only about 3.7% of them were.

Compared Random Forest, Extra Trees, and Gradient Boosted Trees on balanced and imbalanced training data, focusing on recall and F1 since missing a distressed company costs a lender far more than a false alarm.
